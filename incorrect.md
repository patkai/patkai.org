## An Incorrect Theory of Software Development

Software development has always been a bit of a mistery to me. Well, first magic, then mister, to be exact. I think I
was 9 years old - that takes us back to 1982 - when my mother told me that at her workplace there are some CNC machines
and the men in the factory "program" them. Once the programming is done, they sit down with a cup of coffee and wait until
the machines do the machining job. At the end of the day the men pick up the shiny little objects and - job well done - go
home to their families, taking with them a comfortable salary.
