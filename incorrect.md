## An Incorrect Theory of Software Development

### Smitten

Software development has always been a bit of a mistery to me. Well, first magic, then mistery, to be exact. I think I
was 9 years old - that takes us back to 1982 - when my mother told me that at her workplace there are some CNC machines
and the men in the factory "program" them. Once the programming is done, they sit down with a cup of coffee and wait
until the machines do the job. At the end of the day the men pick up a bunch of shiny little objects, and - job well
done - go home to their families, taking with them a comfortable salary. When 
I went to sleep that night I was dreaming of becoming a programmer. I had a machine that I could tell to do work for me,
I had a nice cup of coffee in my hand and I was casually talking to my best friend - a fellow programmer - while the 
machine was humming away. At the end of the day I stood up, stretched a little an walked to my car. I think it was a 
Lada 1600s, which seemed to be a realistic dream car at that time. 

I couldn't get over it, and I still can't.
